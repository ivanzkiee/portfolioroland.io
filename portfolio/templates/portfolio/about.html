Hello, I'm Roland Ivan M. Palasigue

I am a fresh graduate with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from STI College Muñoz-EDSA, Quezon City, with a strong passion for web development, software engineering, and technology innovation. I am eager to begin my professional career in the IT industry, where I can apply my knowledge, strengthen my technical and problem-solving skills, and contribute to impactful projects in a dynamic work environment.

My academic journey began with a background in Mobile Application and Web Development (2020–2022), where I built a solid foundation in programming and web technologies. This foundation led to the development of my capstone project, TenantFlow, a comprehensive web-based rental management system. This project showcased my full-stack development skills using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Django, and allowed me to gain hands-on experience in frontend design, backend development, system testing, and documentation.

In addition, I completed my On-the-Job Training at the National Printing Office from March 2026 to May 2026, where I gained practical experience in an office and IT support environment. My responsibilities included scanning and organizing documents, managing digital file records, assisting with basic troubleshooting of computer systems, and supporting administrative and technical tasks. This experience enhanced my attention to detail, adaptability, and ability to work efficiently in a professional setting.

I have technical proficiency in multiple programming languages and technologies, including Java, C#, SQL, Android development, and web development tools such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Django. I am also skilled in using productivity tools like Microsoft Office 365 and Google Workspace, with additional experience in basic network troubleshooting and hardware/software maintenance.

In addition to my academic background, I have completed an estimated 39+ certificates, trainings, and seminars, including school-issued hardcopy certificates from Senior High School through tertiary education. These include training in cybersecurity, data science, artificial intelligence for business, leadership, and digital citizenship from organizations such as DICT, HP Foundation, Oracle Academy, and Cisco Networking Academy.

I also bring strong leadership and organizational experience through my involvement in the Central Student Government (CSG), where I served as Computer Science Representative, Secretary, and Vice President for External Affairs and Technical Committee Head. In these roles, I managed documentation, coordinated technical requirements for events, and helped organize programs with over 300 participants, demonstrating my ability to lead, collaborate, and execute projects effectively.

I am a proactive and adaptable individual with strong communication, teamwork, and time management skills. I am highly motivated to contribute to a forward-thinking organization while continuously learning and growing as an IT professional.
