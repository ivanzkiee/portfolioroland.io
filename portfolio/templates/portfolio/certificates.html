Gawad Kabataang Pylon - Namumukod Tanging Organisasyon

Organization Recognition

The "Gawad Kabataang Pylon" (Youth Pylon Award) is a prestigious recognition given to outstanding student leaders and organizations who exemplify leadership, service, and commitment to the student community. This recognition highlights my contributions as a student leader through:

  • Leading and supporting student organization initiatives as part of the Central Student Government
  • Coordinating external partnerships, including the collaboration between STI College Muñoz-EDSA CSG and the Quezon City Youth Development Office (QCYDO)
  • Managing partnership requirements, documentation, and accomplishment reports to strengthen organizational collaborations
  • Promoting student welfare, engagement, and meaningful youth development programs
  • Demonstrating leadership, accountability, and commitment to school and community initiatives

This recognition reflects my dedication to youth leadership, organizational development, and creating meaningful partnerships that contribute to a more engaged and empowered student community.
